FLOOR 03 ABOUT — DESKTOP MASTER REVIEW AND RESPONSIVE PLAN

RECOMMENDATION

The proposed direction is feasible and worth retaining: left-wall reading area, the actual portrait in the physical frame, and the corridor on the right. Recommend conditional composition approval, not final artwork or integration approval. Before producing the full family, lock the portrait aperture/inset and prove contrast plus a perspective-correct long-content continuation. The package currently supplies neither unlit light masters nor responsive production plates.

1. WHAT WAS INSPECTED

Read AGENTS.md, current AboutSection.vue, About CSS and artwork documentation, actual biography/toolkit/approach data, SiteNavigation/FloorNavigator, and all package README/geometry guidance. Inspected clean plate, raster proof and responsive references.

The candidate is opaque WebP RGB, 1920x1080, 218,198 bytes (213.1 KiB). download.webp is byte-identical to it. The portrait is 799x730, 61,756 bytes, and byte-identical to public/about/irene-portrait.webp. No portrait regeneration, editing or source re-encoding was performed. The raster proof includes text/photo/03 overlays and is not a deployable background. The clean plate has baked warm lighting and is not an off-state production base. Its native generated resolution was 1672x941, as the README discloses.

Fit mode applies temporary CSS only: left narrative and tabs, 16px body/panel text, 18px intro, an unfiltered photo perspective-mapped to the supplied quad and the existing 03 rail repositioned on the wall. Logical content order and tab state remain unchanged.

3. PORTRAIT, 03 AND COORDINATE RISKS

Use the supplied aperture TL(934,180), TR(1284,204), BR(1283,740), BL(934,771) as the current guide in the 1920x1080 export. Normalized/export values disagree by less than 0.42px per coordinate, consistent with rounding. The corrected temporary overlay visually sits inside the frame. Still obtain an artist-approved inner mask and an explicit inset policy; rounded guide points alone do not certify exact frame occlusion.

The existing portrait is nested inside .about__identity, whose GSAP entrance transform creates a new coordinate containing block even when its matrix has zero translation. A naive absolute photo overlay was offset by the left text origin. Fit mode freezes that ancestor transform and uses a shared positioned host. Later integration should keep the photo plane outside transformed text ancestors or animate text children independently. Image, frame mask and art must share one proportional scene coordinate system and respond to actual wrapper width, including scrollbar and font changes. Never independently center/crop the image and mask with cover.

03 is real HTML in the temporary proof. The proposed normalized box [0.70,0.238,0.08,0.10] clears the portrait and existing floor controls. Existing floor navigation clears content but overlays part of the distant corridor, unlike the small painted-style labels in the raster proof. Reserve its right-side space in production art, or separately approve an About-only compact CSS treatment preserving the same controls. Do not bake navigation or 03 into artwork.

4. READABILITY AND ACCESSIBILITY

The layout is generally readable at normal size. The left greenery reaches the top eyebrow region, and some bright wall flecks sit under body copy. Keep foliage outside the reading guide or adjust the HTML inset locally.

Conservative source-pixel contrast comparison used the actual rendered foreground colours and paragraph bounding boxes against the clean plate, ignoring text shadows. At the 32% guide, the darkest contrast across individual bright box pixels fell to about 2.33:1 for body text; the low 1st percentile was mostly 6.25:1 or higher. The wider trial reached about 1.62:1 at isolated bright pixels. These are background-box statistics, not glyph-level WCAG failures or a conformance certification. They show why visual inspection alone is insufficient. Use a restrained wall-local dark scrim or quiet the bright flakes, then verify actual text/background contrast in all production lighting states. Existing panel backing improves panel contrast but was not numerically certified here.

PASS in the temporary actual-component preview: ArrowRight selected Toolkit and moved focus, End selected Approach, native tab/tabpanel roles and selected state updated, and the existing cyan focus outline remained visible. The supplied photo file was used directly and fit mode removed existing display colour filters to avoid changing appearance. Screen-reader operation, native 200% zoom, physical devices and reduced-motion preference changes were not exercised in this review. Future acceptance must cover them. Standalone navigation controls were checked for geometry/clearance, not full multi-floor routing; other floors were not mounted or changed.

5. RESPONSIVE ARTWORK AND EXACT FUTURE FILES

Desktop: 1920x1080, proportional top-center. Portrait tablet: a separately composed 1536x1280 upper plate. Phone: separately composed 1080x1296 upper plate. These are scene rectangles, not fixed full-page heights. Full biography and tabs remain normal-flow HTML. Landscape can reuse the wide architecture with stacked copy when width/height makes a desktop column unsuitable.

Tablet keeps the intro beside the framed portrait, then biography/tabs below. Phone puts all reading content below the portrait scene. Preserve the approved material palette, frame design, relative viewing height and recognizable corridor; do not demand a pixel-identical crop or reuse coordinates from another profile. Proposed composition zones and exact asset rows are in ARTWORK-REQUIREMENTS.txt and future-assets.json. Actual tablet/mobile frame quads must come from their finished compositions, not invented coordinates here.

Per profile, plan an opaque clean base, full-canvas portrait-aperture SVG, full-canvas alpha emission and wall spill if on/off is retained, and a quiet wall continuation plus bottom floor/endcap and upper retention mask wherever content grows. The perspective corridor needs an authored retention contour: a flat horizontal cut may retain an unwanted floor halfway through the reading area or slice architecture. No old v004 wall texture, 650px cut line, lamp pass or mobile geometry transfers to this new camera.

The JSON specifies exact proposed canvases/formats/anchors/filenames. The floor/endcap uses a full-profile alpha canvas, bottom-anchored, until an approved crop rectangle can be measured. A 512px-high continuation tile is a proposed export period requiring a seam/repetition proof. Floor reflections affected by controlled lamps require a separate conditional bottom-registered illumination pass. They cannot travel with a top-wall light mask when the section grows.

1) Approve composition, inner frame/inset, reading contrast treatment, 03 and navigation clearance; prove long content before bulk derivatives.
2) Produce tablet/mobile masters, lock geometry, derive registered masks/lighting/continuation/endcaps and validate all manifests/alpha/hashes/seams in staging.

Geometry currently lives in AboutSection.vue and can stay there unless its size warrants an About-only data module. Reuse actual content and tab logic; keep photo/03/navigation accessible. No other floors or infrastructure changes.
4) Render one profile-selected, proportional scene host. Align base/portrait/aperture/top lights to it. Keep reading content in flow and bottom floor group independently anchored to actual section bottom. Preserve observer/listener/GSAP cleanup and steady reduced-motion lighting. CSS is sufficient; no Three.js or dependencies needed.
5) Test all six established sizes, every tab, light off/on, longer content, text zoom, keyboard/focus, image failures, forward/reverse navigation and reduced motion. Then run Node 22 build, vue-tsc and npm test. Those checks belong to implementation acceptance, not this source-unchanged review.
