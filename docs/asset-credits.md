# Asset credits and source material

The aquarium uses optimized derivatives of [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/)
assets distributed under CC0: `boulder_01`, `fern_02`, `moss_01`,
`dead_tree_trunk_02`, `coast_land_rocks_03`, `calathea_orbifolia_01` and
`bark_debris_01`. Runtime models and textures are under `public/hero/aquarium/`.
Original 1K glTF packages and Blender working files are preserved locally under
`local-archive/assets/`.

The fish derives from the CC0
[Khronos BarramundiFish sample](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/BarramundiFish).
The optimized runtime copy is `public/hero/aquarium/small-fish.glb`.

Cat recordings retain their individual credits and license links in
`public/audio/cat/credits.html`, which is available from Scene preview.

The black tomcat sprite and mouse Easter egg artwork were generated with the
built-in image generation tool. Original prompts and production notes are
preserved under `local-archive/public/hero/cat/` and `local-archive/docs/`.
Owner-supplied artwork, source references and historical production provenance
are also retained in the local archive.
