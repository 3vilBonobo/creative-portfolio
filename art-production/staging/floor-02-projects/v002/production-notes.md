# Production notes

Assets exported to exact v002 filenames and native export geometry, without upscaling. No floor numbers, UI, or project cards were added. The left magenta and right cyan illumination are baked permanent ambient. The upper-right service fixture is dark in bases, with optional amber emitter and spill PNG overlays.

Important: The desktop and mobile source images were generated separately (with visual coordination), **not** derived from a common native 2x master. They are not exact registered crops of one larger composition. The image-generated assets are only at deployment dimensions. Do not claim genuine 2x detail.

Inspect the actual app at six viewport sizes, one/six/twelve cards, 200% text, dialog focus and light on/off before accepting. Keep previous assets intact.
